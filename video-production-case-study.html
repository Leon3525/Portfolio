Documentary-Style Educational Video Production

Elevating Educational Video Through Collaborative Fieldwork

Overview

Beyond standard technical tutorials and static course media, our Learning Production (LP) team at RMCAD produces immersive, documentary-style educational video assets that bring academic theory into real-world practice. When designing an advanced course on 21st-century museum leadership, we partnered directly with executive directors at two Denver cultural institutions. By executing on-location field shoots and iteratively refining our post-production design between site visits, we created modular video assets that measurably enhanced learner engagement and topic retention.

Discovery & Pedagogical Alignment

Rather than relying strictly on static textbook readings and instructor lecture recordings, the LP team set out to ground abstract leadership concepts in authentic, executive-level dialogue filmed on location in Denver museum spaces.

Institution Focus 01

Museo de las Americas

Centered on community-driven leadership, Latinx and Indigenous heritage, civic responsibility, and addressing the immediate social needs of diverse urban communities.

Institution Focus 02

The Clyfford Still Museum

Centered on single-artist legacy preservation, collections care, and balancing artist intent with modern audience activation and democratic access.

Fieldwork Operations & Team Coordination

Executing field shoots in active museum spaces required dynamic cross-functional coordination between ICDs, LXDs, course SMEs, and museum leadership.

Standardized Shoot Windows

Allocated 30 minutes for setup and breakdown, 30 minutes for executive interviews, and 60 minutes for targeted gallery and behind-the-scenes B-roll collection totalling 2 hours.

Collaborative Crew Roles

ICDs and LXDs shared responsibilities on set—managing lighting grids, audio balancing, framing, and posing as visitors to demonstrate gallery interaction.

Targeted B-Roll Shot Lists

Captured climate-controlled vaults, architectural light wells, archives, works on paper, and interactive visitor spaces to reinforce spoken narrative.

Responsive Growth Between Shoots

Real production growth happens when team reflection immediately transforms post-production design.

Shoot 01: Museo de las Americas

Continuous Narrative Edit

  • Attempted a seamless, uninterrupted story cut without visual editorial prompts.
  • Post-Shoot Insight: While content was rich, learners struggled to identify transitions between distinct complex leadership topics.
  • Prompted the team to re-evaluate structural signposting for higher education video.
Shoot 02: Clyfford Still Museum

Visible Editorial Signposts

  • Introduced visible on-screen question callouts between core thematic interview sections.
  • Learner Outcome: Explicit visual signposts allowed students to easily track transitions from collections care to community activation.
  • Paired structural breaks with rhythmic B-roll cutaways for higher production polish.
