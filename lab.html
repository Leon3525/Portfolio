Data Analysis In The Lab

Select a methodology below to examine the analysis framework

Click either button above or select an image card to display its corresponding methodology description.

Qualitative analysis explores meaning, context, and lived experience within research. Interviews and observations often reveal insights numbers alone cannot capture. Researchers interpret themes and narratives to better understand human behavior. Open-ended inquiry allows unexpected patterns to emerge.

Quantitative analysis focuses on measurable variables and statistical relationships. Researchers use numerical models to identify trends and test hypotheses. Large datasets allow analysts to compare patterns across populations. Structured methods support repeatability and precision in findings.
