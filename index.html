What makes me different?

I believe in writing learning content that is completely relatable. By tapping into the "If I wouldn't do it, I wouldn't ask my learners to do it" mindset, I focus on crafting real-world scenarios, interactive components, and thought-provoking media that bypass standard, uninspiring template check-boxes.

Instead of building massive, months-long courses, I specialize in jumping onto your short-term contracts to deliver high-quality, high-impact assets on quick timelines.

Visual Design

With a Bachelor of Fine Art (BFA) degree and a background in User Interface Design, I have years of real-world experience. When creating learning experiences I have a keen eye for UI design, layout, typography, color and accessibility. I can create custom imagery, icons and graphics to take your learning from mid, to magnificent.

Instructional Design

As a licensed education professional with a Master of Arts in Teaching (MAT) degree, I have sound knowledge of adult learning theory, multimedia principles and instructional design models allowing me to create effective, interactive learning which supports learner engagement and retention.

Digital Learning Development

Bringing storyboards to life brings me satisfaction! I specialize in Articulate Rise and Storyline, I can flex my skillset to meet your needs. It is my aim to push the software to its limits, to create courses that are exciting while keeping accessibility at the forefront of my process.
